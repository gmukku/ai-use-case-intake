# PTO and Leave of Absence Requests

*Northbridge Group internal SOP. Synthetic document for demonstration.*

## Purpose

Give employees and managers one consistent process for time off, and make sure legally
protected leaves are identified and handled correctly.

## PTO

- Full-time employees accrue 1.25 days per month (15 days per year); part-time employees
  accrue pro rata by scheduled hours. Accrual appears in the HRIS on the first of each month.
- Requests are submitted in the HRIS at least 5 business days ahead for 3 or more consecutive
  days. Managers approve or decline in the HRIS; HR does not approve PTO.
- Up to 5 unused days carry over into the next calendar year. Carryover above that is
  forfeited on 31 December.
- Negative balances are not permitted except by written HR exception.

## Leave of absence

1. An employee who expects to be out more than 5 consecutive days for a medical, family, or
   military reason contacts HR (not only their manager). Managers who become aware of such a
   situation notify HR within 2 business days.
2. HR determines eligibility: FMLA requires 12 months of service and 1,250 hours worked in the
   prior 12 months at a location with 50 or more employees within 75 miles. State leave laws
   may provide additional or different rights; HR checks the "State Employment Requirements"
   guide.
3. HR sends the eligibility notice and any required certification forms within 5 business
   days of learning of the need for leave.
4. Approved leave is recorded in the HRIS with start and expected end dates. Benefits
   continuation and premium payment arrangements are confirmed in writing.
5. HR checks in two weeks before the expected return date to confirm return or extension.

## Common employee questions HR answers

- How many PTO days do I have? (HRIS balance, updated monthly)
- Can I take unpaid time off when my PTO is exhausted? (Manager discretion, HR exception)
- Does bereavement or jury duty use PTO? (No: separate paid leave categories, see handbook
  sections 4.3 and 4.4)

## Systems

HRIS (accruals, requests, leave records), employee handbook and state guide in SharePoint,
HR help desk queue for questions.
