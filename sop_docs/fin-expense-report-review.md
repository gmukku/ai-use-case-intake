# Expense Report Review and Reimbursement

*Northbridge Group internal SOP. Synthetic document for demonstration.*

## Purpose

Reimburse legitimate business expenses quickly while enforcing policy consistently.

## Policy limits (summary)

| Category | Limit | Receipt required |
|---|---|---|
| Meals, traveling alone | $60 per day | Itemized receipt above $25 |
| Meals with clients | $100 per person | Itemized receipt and attendee names, always |
| Lodging | Booked through the travel tool; rate exceptions need pre-approval | Always |
| Air travel | Economy; upgrades not reimbursable | Always |
| Ground transport | Actual cost; personal mileage at the IRS standard rate | Above $25 |
| Software or equipment | Not reimbursable; use procurement | n/a |

Alcohol is reimbursable only for client meals. Expenses submitted more than 60 days after
the date incurred are not reimbursed without VP approval.

## Procedure

1. Employees submit reports in the expense tool within 30 days of the expense, with receipts
   attached and a business purpose on every line.
2. The manager approves in the tool. Managers may not approve their own reports.
3. AP reviews every report over $500, and a 20% random sample of reports under $500, for
   policy compliance: limits, receipts, duplicates, personal items, correct cost center.
4. Exceptions are returned to the employee with the specific line and policy reference. The
   employee has 5 business days to correct or justify.
5. Approved reports are paid in the next weekly reimbursement run (Fridays) via direct deposit.

## Frequent questions AP answers

- Is a hotel minibar charge reimbursable? (No, personal.)
- Can I expense a team lunch? (Yes, as a "team meal" up to $40 per person with attendee
  names; needs manager pre-approval above 10 people.)
- What if I lost the receipt? (Missing-receipt affidavit for items under $75; above that,
  request a duplicate from the merchant.)

## Systems

Expense management tool (submission, approvals, receipts), corporate card feed, ERP (posting
and cost centers), payroll direct-deposit file for reimbursements.
