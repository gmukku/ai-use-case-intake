# RFP Response Process

*Northbridge Group internal SOP. Synthetic document for demonstration.*

## Purpose

Respond only to RFPs worth winning, and respond to those consistently and on time using
approved, reusable answers.

## Step 1: Go / no-go (within 2 business days of receipt)

The account executive completes the qualification checklist: relationship with the buyer,
fit with product capabilities, budget confirmed, competitive position, timeline feasibility.
The regional sales director decides go / no-go. No-go RFPs get a courteous decline.

## Step 2: Staffing and plan

- AE: owner and executive summary
- Solutions engineer: technical and security sections
- Sales operations: coordination, formatting, submission
- Legal: contract terms and exceptions (engaged at least 5 business days before deadline)
- Finance: pricing approval per the discount matrix

A response plan lists every question, its owner, and an internal due date at least 3
business days before the buyer's deadline.

## Step 3: Drafting

- Start from the **answer library** (enablement platform): approved answers tagged by topic,
  last-reviewed date, and product version. Answers older than 12 months must be re-verified
  by the owning team before reuse.
- New answers are drafted by the section owner and added to the library after the response is
  submitted, tagged and dated.
- Never paste marketing copy in place of a direct answer. Answer the question asked, then
  add supporting detail.

## Step 4: Review gates

1. Section owners self-review against the question wording.
2. Solutions engineering reviews all technical claims for accuracy against the current
   release.
3. Legal reviews exceptions and any commitments (SLAs, data handling, liability).
4. Regional director signs off on pricing and the executive summary.

## Step 5: Submission and retrospective

Sales operations submits through the buyer's portal or email with confirmation retained.
Within a week of the decision, the AE records the outcome and any buyer feedback; lost RFPs
get a 30-minute retrospective.

## Systems

CRM (opportunity and RFP record), enablement platform (answer library), shared drive
(working documents), e-signature tool, legal request queue.
