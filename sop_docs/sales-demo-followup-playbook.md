# Post-Demo Follow-Up Playbook

*Northbridge Group internal SOP. Synthetic document for demonstration.*

## Purpose

Every demo gets a consistent, timely follow-up so prospects keep momentum and the pipeline
reflects reality.

## Timing

- Follow-up email: within 24 hours of the demo (same business day preferred).
- CRM update: same day as the demo.
- Next step on the calendar before the follow-up is sent whenever possible.

## Follow-up email structure

1. **Thank-you and recap** (2-3 sentences): what the prospect said their problem is, in
   their words.
2. **What they saw**: the 2-3 capabilities demonstrated, each tied to the problem above.
3. **Objections and answers**: anything raised on the call, with the answer or the commitment
   to get one (owner and date).
4. **Next step**: a proposed date and time, or the agreed next step if already booked.
5. **Attachments**: only the collateral relevant to what was discussed; never the full
   product deck.

Tone: plain, specific, no marketing adjectives. Under 250 words.

## CRM update (required fields)

- Opportunity stage moved to "Demo completed"
- Call summary: attendees, problem statement, capabilities shown, objections, next step
- Next-step date and owner
- Competitor mentioned (if any)
- Decision process and timeline, if learned

## Sources for the follow-up

- Call recording and transcript (call recording tool)
- The AE's notes
- Approved messaging and objection-handling library (enablement platform)

## Tracking

Sales operations reports weekly on demos without a follow-up logged within 24 hours and on
opportunities in "Demo completed" with no next-step date. Managers review both in the
Monday pipeline meeting.

## Systems

CRM (opportunity, activities), call recording and transcription tool, sales engagement tool
(email sending and sequences), enablement content platform, calendar.
