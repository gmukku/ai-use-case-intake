# Quarterly Business Review Preparation

*Northbridge Group internal SOP. Synthetic document for demonstration.*

## Purpose

Give each managed account a consistent, evidence-based review of value delivered and risks
ahead, and surface renewal and expansion conversations early.

## Cadence and ownership

- Every managed account (enterprise and mid-market tiers) gets a QBR each quarter; the CSM
  owns it. Accounts within 120 days of renewal get the QBR at least 60 days before renewal.
- Preparation starts 2 weeks before the meeting; the internal review happens 3 business days
  before.

## Data to gather

1. **Usage and adoption** from product analytics: active users, feature adoption versus the
   success plan, trend versus last quarter.
2. **Support history** from the help desk: ticket volume by priority, open escalations,
   average resolution time, notable incidents.
3. **Health score** from the customer success platform, with the drivers behind any change.
4. **Commercial context** from the CRM: contract terms, renewal date, open opportunities,
   executive sponsor.
5. **Prior QBR** actions and whether they were completed.

## Standard deck sections

1. Summary of the quarter (one slide, plain language)
2. Goals from the success plan and progress against each
3. Adoption and usage highlights, with one chart per key metric
4. Support and incidents: what happened, what changed as a result
5. Product updates relevant to this customer's goals
6. Risks and recommendations
7. Next quarter's plan and owners

## Internal review

The CSM's manager and the account's AE review the deck. The review checks that every claim
has a data source, that risks are stated plainly, and that any expansion or renewal ask is
positioned but not oversold.

## After the meeting

Within 2 business days: notes and agreed actions recorded in the customer success platform;
health score adjusted if the meeting changed the picture; any product feedback filed.

## Systems

Customer success platform (health, success plans, notes), product analytics, help desk,
CRM, presentation template in the shared drive.
