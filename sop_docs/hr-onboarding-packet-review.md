# New Hire Onboarding Packet Review

*Northbridge Group internal SOP. Synthetic document for demonstration.*

## Purpose

Ensure every new hire's paperwork is complete, correct, and filed before payroll's cutoff for
the first pay run, and that federal employment-eligibility verification is completed on time.

## Scope

All US-based hires. Applies to HR Operations coordinators; hiring managers and the payroll
specialist have supporting responsibilities.

## Required documents

| Document | Who completes | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Form I-9 Section 1 | New hire | On or before first day of work for pay |
| Form I-9 Section 2 | HR coordinator (or delegated manager) after inspecting original documents | Within 3 business days of the first day of work for pay |
| Form W-4 | New hire | Before first pay run |
| State withholding form (where the state requires one) | New hire | Before first pay run |
| Direct deposit authorization with voided check or bank letter | New hire | Before first pay run |
| Employee handbook and policy acknowledgements | New hire | First week |

## Procedure

1. The onboarding tool notifies the coordinator when the new hire submits their packet.
2. Open the packet and check every required document against the table above.
3. For Section 2, schedule the in-person or authorized-remote document inspection with the
   new hire on day one; record the document titles, numbers, and expiration dates exactly as
   shown. Do not accept photocopies for Section 2.
4. If anything is missing, blank, or inconsistent (name mismatch between forms, missing
   signature, expired ID), email the new hire the same day using the "Missing onboarding
   items" template, copy the hiring manager, and set a follow-up reminder for two business days.
5. When complete, mark the packet "Verified" in the onboarding tool and file the signed PDFs
   in the HR SharePoint library under the employee's folder.
6. Notify the payroll specialist that the packet is verified. Payroll will not process a first
   check for an employee whose packet is not marked Verified.

## Controls

- Coordinators log the Section 2 completion date; HR Ops reviews late completions monthly.
- State-specific requirements (e.g. additional notices for certain states) are checked
  against the "State Employment Requirements" guide in SharePoint.
- Packets are audited quarterly on a 10% sample.

## Systems

Onboarding tool (packet intake and status), HR SharePoint library (filed documents,
checklists, state guide), payroll system (first pay run gate), Outlook (chase emails).
