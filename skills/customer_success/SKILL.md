# Customer Success / Support

Illustrative department profile for grounding discovery examples. Synthetic; not tied to any
real organization. Body headings mirror the discovery canvas categories.

## Classification hints

- Tickets, support queue, SLAs, escalations, first-response time, CSAT
- Customer onboarding and implementation (post-sale), training, adoption
- Renewals, churn risk, health scores, expansion, QBRs
- Knowledge base articles, macros, help center content
- Product feedback loops, bug reports relayed to engineering

## Key stakeholders

- Support agents and tier-2 specialists working the queue
- Customer success managers owning a book of accounts
- Onboarding and implementation specialists
- Support managers tracking SLAs and staffing
- Product and engineering as recipients of feedback and bug reports
- Sales for renewals and expansion handoffs
- Customers as the people on the other end of every ticket

## Key activities

- Triaging incoming tickets by urgency and routing to the right team
- Drafting replies from knowledge base articles and past resolutions
- Writing knowledge base articles from recurring resolutions
- Preparing QBR decks and account health summaries
- Monitoring accounts for churn signals and flagging them for outreach
- Typical cadence: continuous for the ticket queue; weekly for account reviews; quarterly for QBRs and renewals

## Value proposition

- First-response and resolution time against SLA
- Ticket deflection through self-service content
- Agent time per ticket and consistency of answers
- Renewal rate and early detection of at-risk accounts
- Time to prepare a QBR or health summary

## System integrations

- Help desk and ticketing: Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk, Salesforce Service Cloud
- Customer success platforms: Gainsight, ChurnZero, Vitally, Planhat
- Knowledge base: Zendesk Guide, Confluence, Notion, Help Scout Docs
- Product analytics: Pendo, Amplitude, Mixpanel
- Communication: Slack Connect, email, in-app chat
- CRM for account context: Salesforce, HubSpot
- Engineering tracker for bugs: Jira, Linear

## Input source

- Knowledge base and help center articles
- Historical ticket resolutions and macros
- Product release notes and documentation
- Account records: contract terms, renewal dates, usage data
- Customer call notes and QBR history

## Input types

- Typed prompts: "how do I answer this ticket?", "which accounts look at risk this month?"
- Document uploads: ticket exports, call notes, contracts
- Images: customer screenshots of errors
- Structured data: usage and health-score exports
- Ask for samples: ten recent tickets with their final resolutions; one recent QBR deck

## Output format

- Q&A chatbot over the knowledge base with article citations
- Drafting into a template: ticket replies in the support voice, KB articles from resolutions, QBR summaries
- Summarization: condensing a long ticket thread or a call into the account record
- Workflow automation: triage and routing suggestions with an agent confirming
- Comparison: what a customer asked versus what the documentation says is supported
- Pulling data from a source: extracting bug details from a ticket into an engineering issue (agent-reviewed)
