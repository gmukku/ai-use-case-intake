# HR / People Operations

Illustrative department profile for grounding discovery examples. Synthetic; not tied to any
real organization. Body headings mirror the discovery canvas categories.

## Classification hints

- Mentions of new hires, onboarding, offboarding, recruiting, candidates, job requisitions
- Benefits enrollment, open enrollment, leave of absence, PTO, FMLA, ADA accommodations
- Payroll *inputs* (timesheets, W-4, direct deposit, I-9); payroll *processing* itself may be Finance
- Employee handbook, policies, performance reviews, engagement surveys, HRIS
- Compliance: EEO, OSHA logs, state-specific employment requirements, audits

## Key stakeholders

- HR generalists and HR operations coordinators who run day-to-day processes
- Recruiters and talent acquisition partners
- Benefits administrators and leave specialists
- Payroll specialists who depend on clean HR data before each pay run
- Hiring managers waiting on HR to complete a step
- New hires and employees as the end recipients of the process
- HR business partners and the CHRO for policy and escalation

## Key activities

- Reviewing onboarding packets for completeness (I-9, W-4, direct deposit, policy acknowledgements)
- Screening inbound applications against a job requisition and scheduling interviews
- Processing benefits enrollment changes and life events during open enrollment
- Answering repetitive employee questions about PTO, leave, and policy
- Preparing offboarding checklists and exit paperwork
- Typical cadence: daily for onboarding and inquiries; seasonal spikes for open enrollment; monthly or quarterly for compliance reporting

## Value proposition

- Time per packet or per inquiry (e.g. 20-30 minutes of manual checking per onboarding packet)
- Error rate on compliance-sensitive forms that causes downstream payroll or audit problems
- Time-to-hire or time-to-productivity for new employees
- Deflection rate on repetitive employee questions
- Consistency of policy answers across HR team members

## System integrations

- HRIS / HCM: Workday, BambooHR, ADP Workforce Now, UKG, Rippling
- Applicant tracking: Greenhouse, Lever, Workable
- Onboarding and e-signature: DocuSign, Adobe Sign, native HRIS onboarding modules
- Benefits administration: Employee Navigator, bswift, carrier portals
- Payroll: ADP, Paylocity, Gusto (often owned jointly with Finance)
- Learning and compliance: Mineral, Cornerstone, KnowBe4
- Ticketing or help desk for HR inquiries: Zendesk, Jira Service Management, ServiceNow HR
- Collaboration: Microsoft Teams, Slack, Outlook for the chasing and reminder emails

## Input source

- Policy documents and the employee handbook in SharePoint or Google Drive
- State-by-state employment requirement guides and internal checklists
- HRIS employee records and org charts
- Benefits plan summaries (SPDs) and carrier documents
- Prior tickets or email threads with common employee questions

## Input types

- Document uploads: scanned or e-signed PDFs (I-9, W-4, offer letters), resumes, benefits forms
- Typed prompts: "is this packet complete?", "how many PTO days does a part-time employee accrue?"
- Images: photos of ID documents, screenshots of HRIS screens
- Ask for samples: two clean onboarding packets and two with missing fields; a handful of real employee questions

## Output format

- Completeness checklist per packet, flagging missing or inconsistent fields
- Q&A chatbot over policy and handbook content with citations to the source document
- Drafting into a template: offer letters, onboarding welcome emails, policy acknowledgement reminders
- Summarization: condensing a resume against a requisition; summarizing exit interviews
- Workflow automation: chase-email generation when items are missing, with a human sending it
- Pulling data from a source: extracting form fields into a structured record for HRIS entry (human-reviewed)
