# Sales / Revenue

Illustrative department profile for grounding discovery examples. Synthetic; not tied to any
real organization. Body headings mirror the discovery canvas categories.

## Classification hints

- Leads, prospects, pipeline, opportunities, deals, quota, territories
- Demos, discovery calls, follow-up emails, proposals, quotes, RFP responses
- CRM data entry and hygiene, call notes, activity logging
- Commissions and comp plans (often shared with Finance)
- Sales enablement content, battlecards, objection handling

## Key stakeholders

- Account executives and sales development representatives doing the outreach
- Sales managers tracking pipeline and coaching
- Sales operations or revenue operations maintaining the CRM and reporting
- Solutions engineers supporting demos and technical questions
- Marketing as the source of inbound leads and content
- Prospects and customers as recipients of outreach
- Finance for quotes, discounts, and commission calculations

## Key activities

- Writing follow-up emails after demos and discovery calls
- Researching a prospect account before a first call
- Logging call notes and next steps into the CRM
- Drafting proposals and responding to RFP questionnaires
- Building quotes with approved discount rules
- Typical cadence: daily for outreach and follow-ups; weekly pipeline reviews; quarterly for forecasts and comp

## Value proposition

- Time from demo to follow-up sent, and follow-up consistency across reps
- Hours per week reps spend on CRM data entry instead of selling
- RFP turnaround time and win rate
- Data quality in the CRM (completeness of key fields)
- Quote accuracy and discount policy compliance

## System integrations

- CRM: Salesforce, HubSpot, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales
- Sales engagement: Outreach, Salesloft, Apollo
- Call recording and notes: Gong, Chorus, Fireflies
- Quoting and contracts: Salesforce CPQ, DealHub, PandaDoc, DocuSign
- Enablement content: Highspot, Seismic, Google Drive
- Calendar and email: Google Workspace, Microsoft 365
- Data enrichment: ZoomInfo, LinkedIn Sales Navigator

## Input source

- CRM opportunity and account records
- Call transcripts and recordings
- Product documentation, pricing sheets, and approved messaging
- Past proposals and RFP answer libraries
- Prospect websites, press releases, public filings

## Input types

- Typed prompts: "draft a follow-up for the demo I just did with Acme", "what objections came up on this call?"
- Document uploads: RFP documents, existing proposals, call transcripts
- Structured data: CRM exports, lead lists
- Ask for samples: two recent demo transcripts and the follow-ups the rep actually sent; one completed RFP

## Output format

- Drafting into a template: follow-up emails, proposal sections, RFP answers in the house style
- Summarization: call summary with next steps and objections for the CRM
- Pulling data from a source: extracting action items and contacts from a transcript into CRM fields (rep-reviewed)
- Q&A chatbot over product docs, pricing, and past RFP answers
- Comparison: prospect requirements versus product capabilities
- Workflow automation: pre-call research brief assembled from public sources
